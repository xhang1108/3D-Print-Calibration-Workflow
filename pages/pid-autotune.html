PID Autotune

What is PID Autotune?

PID autotune tests and adjusts settings to keep a system stable and accurate automatically.

Why is it Important?

Stable temperatures: During printing for consistent extrusion

Reduced fluctuations: Better temperature control and stability

Print quality: More reliable and consistent results

Energy efficiency: Optimized heating control saves power

How to Perform PID Autotune

Connect to your printer via USB or OctoPrint

Ensure the hotend is at room temperature

Send the PID autotune command (e.g., M303 E0 S200 C8 for hotend)

Wait for the process to complete (may take 10-15 minutes)

Save the new PID values to EEPROM

Tips

Perform PID autotune for both hotend and bed

Re-tune after changing nozzles or making significant changes to your printer

Ensure proper cooling during the tuning process
