Max Flow Calibration

What is Max Flow?

Max Flow is the maximum rate at which your printer can extrude filament while maintaining print quality. It's crucial for optimizing print speed and quality.

Calibration Process

Design or download a max flow test model

Start with conservative flow rates

Print the test model, increasing flow rates gradually

Observe print quality and look for signs of under-extrusion

Identify the highest flow rate that maintains good quality

Set your slicer's max volumetric speed accordingly

Factors Affecting Max Flow

Nozzle diameter

Hotend design

Filament type and quality

Extruder capabilities

Print temperature
