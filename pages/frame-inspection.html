Frame Inspection

Why is Frame Inspection Important?

A properly aligned and stable frame is crucial for achieving high-quality 3D prints. Frame inspection helps identify and correct issues that could affect print quality.

Inspection Steps

Check frame stability and ensure all bolts and screws are tight, look for any wobbling or movement

Verify frame squareness using a square tool and measure diagonals to ensure they are equal

Inspect belts and pulleys, check belt tension and ensure proper alignment

Examine the print bed for levelness and ensure smooth movement along all axes

Tools Needed

Square tool

Measuring tape or ruler

Allen wrenches

Screwdriver set

Tips

Perform frame inspection regularly, especially after moving the printer

Address any issues immediately to prevent print quality problems

Consider using vibration dampers to improve stability
