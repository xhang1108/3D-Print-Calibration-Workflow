Bed Calibration

Why is Bed Calibration Important?

Leveling the bed ensures a more accurate reference point for 3D printing.

Calibration Steps

Heat the bed to your usual printing temperature

Use the printer's leveling system (manual or automatic)

Adjust the four corners of the bed

Check the center of the bed

Print a test pattern to verify calibration

Tips

Use a feeler gauge or paper for manual leveling

Perform bed calibration regularly

Consider using a glass bed for a flatter surface

Clean the bed surface before calibration
